3/*
4 * WHAT: Prometheus export for the RPM/dnf pull-through mirror (phase-104
5 * D15.9).
6 * WHY: a package mirror is judged on two numbers: how much of the dnf
7 * traffic it absorbed without touching the WAN, and whether any
8 * metadata came back that did not hash to the checksum its own name
9 * carries. The first is the request family split by route class — the
10 * repomd/metadata/package split is exactly the split an operator sizing
11 * a mirror cares about — and the second is the verify counter, which
12 * should read zero forever and is an alert the day it does not.
13 * HOW: one two-label family (class x outcome, both fixed enums — INVARIANT
14 * #8) plus one scalar, through the shared writer helpers.
15 */
