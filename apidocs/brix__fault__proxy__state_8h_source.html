1/*
2 * brix_fault_proxy_state.h — the brix-fault-proxy program's shared lever state.
3 *
4 * WHAT: The fault levers, connection-scoped switches, attack-mock config, and
5 * traffic counters that every TU of the brix-fault-proxy binary reads or
6 * writes, plus the prototypes of the functions those TUs hand to each
7 * other (relay data path, control-command parsers, CLI parsing).
8 *
9 * WHY: brix_fault_proxy.c crossed the 600-line cap by a wide margin
10 * (coding-standards §1) and was split into per-stage TUs. The state it
11 * carried was already program-global — one process-wide set of levers is
12 * the whole point of a live-controllable fault proxy — so nothing new is
13 * introduced here: the same objects simply became visible to the TUs that
14 * were carved out of the same file. `brix_fault_proxy.c` remains their
15 * single definition site.
16 *
17 * HOW: Include after the module headers (brix_fault_lever.h et al). Levers are
18 * `volatile` and read lock-free from relay threads; anything wider than a
19 * word (mutation buffers, triggers, TLS/HTTP config) is mutated under
20 * g_ext_lock and snapshotted into the hot path. Counters are bumped with
21 * the CBUMP macros (relaxed atomics), never assigned directly.
22 */
