4/*
5 * http_cache_fill_internal.h - private glue shared by the three
6 * http_cache_fill translation units (split mechanically from a single file
7 * for auditability; zero behaviour change):
8 *
9 * http_cache_fill.c public entry + pool resolver (+ !NGX_THREADS stub)
10 * http_cache_fill_registry.c coalescing waiter registry (attach/detach/hold/abort)

13 * It declares the fill ctx / waiter structs, the per-worker in-flight list, and
14 * the handful of symbols DEFINED in one unit but REFERENCED from another. All of
