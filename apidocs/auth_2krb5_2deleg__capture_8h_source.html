6/*
7 * deleg_capture.h — inbound XrdSeckrb5 forwarded-TGT delegation-capture state
8 * machine (phase-70 §5.7).
9 *
10 * WHAT: The server-side two-round exchange that turns a verified krb5 login into
11 * a forwardable initiator credential for the outbound origin leg. Round 1
12 * (in auth.c) verifies the AP_REQ, and — when brix_krb5_delegate is on —
13 * parks the round-1 session subkey + client principal (brix_krb5_deleg_park)
14 * and replies kXR_authmore "fwdtgt" (brix_krb5_send_fwdtgt) instead of
15 * finalizing. Round 2 receives the client's krb5_fwd_tgt_creds() KRB_CRED,
16 * decrypts it with the parked subkey, and serialises the captured TGT to a
17 * fresh 0600 FILE ccache whose path is stashed on ctx->krb5.ccache
18 * (brix_krb5_deleg_capture).
19 *
20 * WHY: The origin auth engine (brix_cache_origin_auth_krb5) and the async-safe
21 * cred carry (carry.c) are already live; this is the missing front-door
22 * CAPTURE that feeds them. Its output (the parked ccache) is consumed at
23 * request time by brix_root_vfs_bind_session → brix_vfs_deleg_set_krb5, so the
24 * forwarded TGT re-authenticates the backend leg AS the inbound user.
25 *
26 * The capture crypto (brix_krb5_capture_fwd_cred) and the FILE-ccache export
27 * (brix_krb5_cred_to_ccache) are proven live vs a real MIT KDC by
28 * tests/test_krb5_forward_live.py; the synchronous seams here (the gate, the
29 * round-2 payload framing, the origin-SPN derivation) are unit-tested by
30 * tests/c/krb5_deleg_capture_test.c. All handles stay opaque behind void* on
31 * brix_ctx_krb5_t so krb5.h never leaks into the widely-included ctx header.
32 */
