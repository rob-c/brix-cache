1/*
2 * rpm.h — the RPM/dnf pull-through mirror plane: config, request ctx, seams.
3 *
4 * WHAT: the location config and per-request context for `src/protocols/rpm/`,
5 * plus the handler entry point and the prototypes its merge / gate /
6 * serve files share.
7 * WHY: an RPM mirror is a cache whose entire policy is one distinction the
8 * file NAMES already encode: `repodata/repomd.xml` is the mutable
9 * freshness root, everything createrepo writes beside it is
10 * `<checksum>-<name>` — immutable AND self-verifying — and packages are
11 * immutable in practice. Expressed as an nginx `proxy_cache` recipe
12 * (the phase-104 D11 deployment note) that distinction can only be
13 * approximated by a TTL, and the checksum in the file name cannot be
14 * checked at all. Expressed here it is exact: the classifier decides the
15 * freshness window, and the cache tier's third self-addressing verify
16 * mode (rpm-repodata) hashes every metadata fill against the digest its
17 * own name carries before the bytes are ever served.
18 * HOW: the shared preamble is the FIRST member of the loc conf (the tree-wide
19 * convention brix_http_common_adopt / ngx_http_brix_shared_merge rely
20 * on), the surface flag defaults off, and nothing here allocates: a
21 * location without brix_rpm_mirror never grows a handler, so a
22 * non-RPM deployment is structurally untouched.
23 */
