1/*
2 * oci_module_internal.h — seams internal to src/protocols/oci/.
3 *
4 * WHAT: the declarations the OCI module's own translation units share and
5 * nobody outside the directory may call: the merge orchestrator the
6 * module context installs, the finalize observer that charges the
7 * metric families exactly once, the guard-line emitter, and the small
8 * response helpers the gate and the mirror handler both need.
9 * WHY: oci.h is the plane's PUBLIC face (the handler entry point, the loc
10 * conf, the classified ctx) — it is included from ./config-listed
11 * siblings such as the metrics exporter. Keeping the intra-module
12 * wiring out of it means a change to the file split never ripples
13 * past this directory, which is exactly how src/protocols/cvmfs/
14 * separates cvmfs.h from cvmfs_module_internal.h.
15 * HOW: declarations only; every definition lives in the .c named in the
16 * comment above it.
17 */
