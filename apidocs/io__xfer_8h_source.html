1/* File: tpc io_xfer — the blocking full-transfer byte loop, free of nginx
2 * WHAT: One primitive, brix_tpc_xfer_all(), that moves exactly `len` bytes in
3 * one direction over either a raw blocking fd or an OpenSSL session.
4 *
5 * WHY: tpc_send_all() and tpc_recv_exact() in io.c were byte-for-byte the same
6 * loop with send/recv and SSL_write/SSL_read swapped — 25 duplicated
7 * lines carrying the EINTR rule, the <=0 rule and the INT_MAX clamp in
8 * two places, so a fix to one silently left the other wrong. Extracting
9 * it also lifts it out of nginx's include graph: this header needs only
10 * OpenSSL and libc, so the loop can be compiled and driven over a
11 * socketpair by a standalone unit test (io_xfer_unittest.c) instead of
12 * being reachable only through a live TPC pull.
13 *
14 * HOW: the caller passes the already-resolved SSL* (NULL for cleartext), so
15 * this TU never sees brix_tpc_pull_t. Direction is a flag rather than a
16 * function pointer: send() takes a const buffer and recv() does not, and
17 * one branch inside the loop is cheaper to read than two shims. */
