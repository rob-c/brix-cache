1/*
2 * client/lib/platform/preload.h - how the POSIX shim stands in for libc
3 *
4 * WHAT: The umbrella the preload shim (client/preload/) includes for the two
5 * things that differ per dynamic linker: what a wrapper is CALLED and
6 * how the real libc function behind it is REACHED. No host conditional
7 * lives here; <host>/preload.h supplies both through the same computed
8 * include the rest of the PAL uses.
9 * WHY: glibc's LD_PRELOAD and dyld's DYLD_INSERT_LIBRARIES interpose in
10 * opposite ways. Under LD_PRELOAD the shim defines `open` itself and
11 * the next definition down the search order is libc's. Under dyld a
12 * library that defined `open` would replace nothing: interposition is
13 * an explicit __interpose table pairing a differently named wrapper
14 * with the libSystem symbol, and the shim's own references stay bound
15 * to libSystem.
16 * HOW: BRIXPOSIX_WRAP(name) the wrapper's identifier
17 * BRIXPOSIX_REAL_RESOLVE(name) an expression yielding libc's function
18 */
