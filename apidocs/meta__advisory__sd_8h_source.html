4/*
5 * meta_advisory_sd.h — the one translation from an SD setattr request to an
6 * advisory-metadata delta.
7 *
8 * WHAT: brix_meta_advisory_from_setattr() maps brix_sd_setattr_t (the storage-
9 * neutral union of kXR_chmod and kXR_setattr) onto brix_meta_advisory_t
10 * (the reserved blob object stores persist and overlay on stat).
11 * WHY: Every backend with no native POSIX metadata — S3/remote, RADOS/ceph,
12 * WebDAV/http — needs exactly this translation, and it is not mechanical:
13 * UTIME_OMIT means "leave it", UTIME_NOW means "stamp wall time", and a
14 * lone (uid_t)-1 or (gid_t)-1 means the owner change CANNOT be represented
15 * at all in a model that carries uid and gid together. Three copies of
16 * that reasoning is three chances to get one of the sentinels wrong, and
17 * getting one wrong writes a bogus owner onto an object.
18 * HOW: A separate TU from meta_advisory.c on purpose: the codec itself is
19 * deliberately dependency-free (libc only) so it unit-tests standalone and
20 * links into libxrdproto; this half needs sd.h for brix_sd_setattr_t, so
21 * it is kept out of the codec rather than dragging sd.h into it.
22 */
