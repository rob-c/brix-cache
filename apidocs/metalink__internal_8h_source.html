1/*
2 * metalink_internal.h — private split contract of the metalink parser.
3 *
4 * WHAT: One concept: the bounded XML lexing primitives the two phase-100
5 * metalink TUs exchange — metalink_lex.c owns tag/attribute/text
6 * extraction and entity decoding; metalink.c owns the document semantics
7 * (ranking, digest folding, file-scope collection, the public API).
8 * WHY: metalink.c alone crossed the 600-line file gate, and the two layers are
9 * genuinely separable: the lexer knows nothing about mirrors or digests,
10 * and the semantics layer never touches a byte offset. Splitting on that
11 * line keeps each TU one concept (coding-standards §1) and gives the
12 * hostile-input lexing rules their own doc surface.
13 * HOW: Both TUs include this after copy_internal.h. Every routine here is
14 * bounded by an explicit `end`/`gt` pointer and never allocates — the
15 * parser's purity contract holds across the split.
16 *
17 * Requires: copy_internal.h before inclusion. Not a public API: include only
18 * from client/lib/xfer/.
19 */
