4/*
5 * service_publish.h — the domain-gated staged-publish verb for SERVICE
6 * storage (phase-108 C10).
7 *
8 * WHAT: brix_service_publish_bytes()/_fd() — the one form every service-storage
9 * site (the OCI registry today; the FRM journal tomorrow) calls to make a
10 * byte string or an already-written staged file appear atomically at a
11 * final path, with the durability class its storage domain demands (§3.3).
12 *
13 * WHY: The OCI registry privately rebuilt staged publish, atomic swap and CAS
14 * presence over raw syscalls with no fsync anywhere — in the one domain
15 * phase 107 marks durable. This verb consolidates that onto the phase-107
16 * staging primitives (staged_file.c) behind the typed domain claim, so a
17 * manifest, tag or blob the client was told was 201 Created is on stable
18 * storage before the answer, the rename is confined, and the durable
19 * publish barrier (C3) actually runs. It sits BESIDE staged_file.c rather
20 * than inside it: staged_file.c stages EXPORT uploads under the phase-105
21 * gate; the domain-aware service arm is a different concern and would
22 * push the file past its 600-line budget.
23 *
24 * HOW: Claim the domain (brix_vfs_domain_claim — EXPORT routes to the phase-105
25 * kernel and answers EROFS, so no export mutation can be laundered through
26 * a service verb), stage beneath req->root_canon with staged_open's
27 * O_EXCL/O_NOFOLLOW/random-suffix/confinement for free, run the EINTR
28 * write loop, fsync the data before the rename when the domain is durable,
29 * then commit through brix_staged_commit{,_excl} — which carries any live
30 * lock record, renames confined, and runs the C3 parent-dir barrier. No
31 * file is left behind on any failure path.
32 */

50 * req->final_path atomically, with the durability class of req->domain (§3.3).
51 * The parent directory of final_path must already exist (the caller owns the
52 * store layout). NGX_OK, or NGX_ERROR with errno:
53 * EROFS — the domain claim refused (an EXPORT-domain publish)
54 * EINVAL — req NULL, domain out of range, or final_path outside root_canon
55 * EEXIST — req->excl and the final path already exists (map to 409/412)
56 * ENOSPC/EIO/EDQUOT — surfaced verbatim from the write, close or fsync
