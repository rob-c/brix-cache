4/* ---- Module: TPC Delegated-Credential Renewal Decision ----
5 *
6 * WHAT: The pure decision half of mid-transfer delegated-credential renewal:
7 * brix_tpc_renew_verdict() answers NOT_NEEDED / DUE / EXPIRED for one
8 * (expires_at, now, lead) triple, and brix_tpc_renew_mode_can_mint()
9 * answers whether a token_mode carries the authority to obtain a FRESH
10 * credential rather than merely forward one it was handed.
11 *
12 * WHY: A multi-hour third-party copy outlives the credential that launched it.
13 * One-shot delegation (phase-70) fetches once at bootstrap and never looks
14 * again, so a pull longer than the token's lifetime streams on a credential
15 * the source may stop honouring mid-flight. The decision is separated from
16 * the wire leg because it is the part that must be exhaustively checkable:
17 * it is pure, it has no I/O, and every branch is a security boundary
18 * (renewing too late is an outage; treating a forwarded token as mintable
19 * would silently re-present a credential the destination never owned).
20 *
21 * HOW: Both entry points are total functions of their arguments with no global
22 * state. The verdict is computed on the REMAINING lifetime (expires_at -
23 * now) evaluated only after the expiry test has established
24 * expires_at > now, so the subtraction can never underflow. Unknown inputs
25 * (no expiry, renewal disabled, a non-positive clock) resolve to
26 * NOT_NEEDED, which is exactly the pre-renewal behaviour — the feature can
27 * only ever add a renewal, never a new refusal, unless the caller opts in
28 * to strict enforcement. Mint authority is a closed allow-list: the two
29 * modes that talk to an issuer (oidc-agent, token-exchange) can mint; every
30 * other mode — including both passthrough forms, "none", the empty string
31 * and any unknown string — cannot.
32 */

42/* Decide whether a delegated credential expiring at `expires_at` needs renewing
43 * at `now`, given a `lead_secs` window before expiry in which renewal is due.
44 * NOT_NEEDED whenever the decision cannot be made or renewal is off:
45 * expires_at <= 0 (no expiry is known), lead_secs <= 0 (renewal disabled), or
46 * now <= 0 (no usable clock). EXPIRED when now >= expires_at; DUE when the
47 * remaining lifetime is at or below lead_secs; NOT_NEEDED otherwise. Pure. */
48brix_tpc_renew_verdict_t brix_tpc_renew_verdict(time_t expires_at, time_t now,
49 time_t lead_secs);

51/* 1 when `token_mode` can obtain a fresh credential from an issuer

54 * mode holds the client's credential, not the authority to re-mint it. Pure. */
