23/* Join gate: block claiming starts when every source has RESOLVED its open
24 * attempt (succeeded or failed), so a fast-opening source cannot drain the
25 * table before a slower sibling ever gets a claim. The grace cap bounds the
26 * wait when a mirror black-holes (SYN drop / tarpit): after this many
27 * milliseconds the opened workers proceed without the stragglers. */
28#define XRDC_XCP_JOIN_GRACE_MS 1000
