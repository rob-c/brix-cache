1/*
2 * ftp_screen.c — data-channel address policy for the GridFTP client.
3 *
4 * WHAT: decide whether the address a passive-mode reply nominated may be dialled.
5 * WHY: a 227/229 reply is attacker-controlled input the moment the control peer
6 * is hostile or compromised: FTP's classic bounce attack is a server that
7 * answers PASV with someone else's address and port, turning the client into
8 * a connector to arbitrary hosts (an SSRF primitive against whatever the
9 * client can reach — link-local metadata services, internal admin ports).
10 * Every serious FTP client therefore ignores the advertised address; curl
11 * calls this --ftp-skip-pasv-ip and has made it the default.
12 * HOW: a pure predicate — no sockets, no DNS — so the rule is exhaustively
13 * unit-testable: the data address must equal the control peer's, and the
14 * port must be unprivileged (a passive data port never legitimately lands
15 * below 1024, whereas 22/25/6379 are exactly what a bounce targets).
16 */
