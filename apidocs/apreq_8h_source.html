6/*
7 * apreq.h — raw-krb5 AP-REQ builder for the OUTBOUND origin leg (phase-70 §5.7).
8 *
9 * WHAT: Given a delegated user's TGT in a FILE ccache PATH (the async-safe carry
10 * artifact brix_krb5_cred_to_ccache produces) and the origin's krb5 service
11 * principal, obtain a service ticket and produce the exact kXR_auth "krb5"
12 * credential payload a stock XRootD krb5 acceptor (libXrdSeckrb5) and brix's
13 * own src/auth/krb5/auth.c both consume: the 5 bytes "krb5\0" (the protocol
14 * name as a NUL-terminated string, per XrdSecInterface) followed by the raw
15 * AP-REQ (ASN.1 [APPLICATION 14]) — byte-for-byte the framing the native
16 * client emits from client/lib/auth/sec/sec_krb5.c.
17 *
18 * WHY: The GSSAPI forwarding engine (forward.c brix_krb5_deleg_negotiate) speaks
19 * gss_init_sec_context tokens, but stock XRootD krb5 is RAW krb5_rd_req — a
20 * dialect mismatch, so the GSS leg can authenticate to no real "&P=krb5"
21 * origin. This builder is the raw-krb5 counterpart: it acts AS the delegated
22 * user with a plain AP-REQ, interoperating with both reference xrootd and
23 * brix acceptors. The delegated credential is already carried onto the fill
24 * task as a ccache PATH, so no GSS re-import is needed on this path.
25 *
26 * HOW: Under BRIX_HAVE_KRB5: krb5_cc_resolve(ccache_path) → krb5_cc_get_principal
27 * (the delegated user) → krb5_parse_name(origin_spn) → krb5_get_credentials
28 * (a service ticket for the origin, off the carried TGT) → krb5_mk_req_extended
29 * (the AP-REQ) → assemble "krb5\0"+AP-REQ into the caller's pool. Per-user
30 * only: any failure fails CLOSED (never a service-credential fallback).
31 * Without BRIX_HAVE_KRB5 the file still compiles and this reports NGX_ERROR.
32 */
