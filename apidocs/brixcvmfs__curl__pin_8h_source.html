1/*
2 * brixcvmfs_curl_pin.h — libcurl name resolution pinned to brix_resolve()
3 * (phase-116).
4 *
5 * WHAT: cvmfs_curl_perform_pinned() performs one transfer, and every
6 * redirect hop of it, with the target host (and the proxy host, when
7 * one is set) resolved by the client's one DNS seam and handed to
8 * libcurl through CURLOPT_RESOLVE, so libcurl's own resolver is never
9 * consulted.
10 * WHY: CURLOPT_URL + CURLOPT_FOLLOWLOCATION are hidden resolver calls:
11 * libcurl would getaddrinfo() the URL host and every Location: hop
12 * itself, bypassing the family hint, the cache and the seam guard.
13 * Walking the redirects by hand keeps each hop's host under the same
14 * pin and the same http/https confinement as the first request.
15 * HOW: the server-side src/net/dns/curl_pin.c design, on the client seam:
16 * pin → CURLOPT_URL → on_hop → perform → CURLINFO_REDIRECT_URL, until
17 * no redirect or the cap; the pin list is unset and freed after each
18 * hop. Lives in the brixcvmfs app because libbrix links no libcurl.
19 */
