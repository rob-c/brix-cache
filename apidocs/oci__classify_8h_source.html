1/* oci_classify.h — the OCI Distribution `/v2/` route classifier (phase-104 D0.2).
2 *
3 * WHAT: turn one decoded request URI into a typed route — which endpoint of the
4 * §0.7.1 matrix it names, and the validated `<name>` / `<reference>` /
5 * `<digest>` / `<session>` spans inside it.
6 * WHY: every downstream decision (method gate, cache key, store path) keys off
7 * the route, and each of those turns wire bytes into path components. One
8 * validating classifier means the grammar is enforced exactly once, at the
9 * edge, and nothing further down has to sanitize: a name/digest that
10 * classifies cannot traverse, by construction (§0.7.2).
11 * HOW: pure C over the shared grammars (shared/oci/{name,digest}.h) — no nginx
12 * types, no allocation, spans point into the caller's URI. That is what
13 * lets the protocol-fuzz lane link this kernel standalone beside the other
14 * parser kernels, the way src/net/guard/ does.
15 */

77/* Classify `uri` (DECODED, NUL-termination not required). Returns 0 with

80 * every span points into it. */
