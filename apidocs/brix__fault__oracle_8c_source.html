43 * copy of the proxy's listen/control/relay sockets. The PAL prefers
44 * close_range() (one syscall for the whole range) over a loop to
45 * RLIMIT_NOFILE, which on this box is ~500k and would cost half a
46 * million syscalls per fork. */

49 _exit(127);
