4/*
5 * mirror_common.h — the two things every pull-through mirror plane shares.
6 *
7 * WHAT: (1) brix_http_mirror_key_path(): compose the on-disk path of a
8 * canonical cache key under an export root. (2)
9 * brix_http_mirror_postconf(): the post-config step an HTTP-ONLY node
10 * needs — ensure the SHM zones and the dashboard a stream{} block would
11 * otherwise have created, then resolve each server's fill thread pool.
12 *
13 * WHY: A mirror is a pure cache node: it has no local export tree, so its
14 * root anchors at "/" and the cache key IS the path. That "the root
15 * contributes nothing" case is easy to get subtly wrong (a leading
16 * double slash, or a cap check that forgets the NUL), and it was written
17 * twice already. The post-config half is worse: a mirror deployment
18 * typically has no stream{} block at all, so the metrics zone and the
19 * dashboard are created here or nowhere — a plane that forgets loses its
20 * observability silently, at runtime, on a node nobody is watching.
21 *
22 * HOW: The postconf helper walks cf's servers, asks the caller's predicate
23 * whether this location's conf actually enables the plane, and resolves
24 * `thread_pool` (or "default") into common->thread_pool, logging a NOTICE
25 * — not an error — when it is absent: a mirror without a pool still
26 * serves, it just fills on the loop. It reaches the shared conf by
27 * casting the module's loc_conf, which is sound because every brix HTTP
28 * protocol declares ngx_http_brix_shared_conf_t as its FIRST member (the
29 * "MUST stay first" comment in each protocol's header).
30 */
