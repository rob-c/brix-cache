3/*
4 * unified_export_vfs.c — scrape-time exporter for the phase-107 VFS-verb
5 * families.
6 *
7 * WHAT: Renders the batch-delete pair (C4), the recall/evict lifecycle pair
8 * (C2), the publish-precondition refusal pair (C6), and the lock-gate
9 * refusal counter (C7) — seven families, each with its zero rows
10 * emitted so a scraper has every series before the first event.
11 * WHY: unified_export.c reached the file-size budget when the C6 pair landed;
12 * the VFS-verb emitters are one cohesive cluster (bounded driver/kind/
13 * result axes over the vfs_* SHM regions), split out the same way the io
14 * families went to unified_export_io.c (coding-standards §1).
15 * HOW: Each emitter reads its region of *shm via brix_metric_value and prints
16 * HELP/TYPE + per-label lines; every axis is a fixed table or
17 * BRIX_FS_ID_COUNT, so labels stay low-cardinality (INVARIANT #8).
