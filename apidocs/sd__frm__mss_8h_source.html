4/*
5 * sd_frm_mss.h — the FRM driver's mass-storage-system (MSS) adapters.
6 *
7 * Two pluggable back ends behind the brix_mss_adapter_t vtable: the built-in
8 * "stub" local-directory tape simulator (test/dev + the default) and the "exec"

30/* Common HEAD every real-HSM adapter context starts with. The exec and lib
31 * adapters share one online-buffer discipline (resolve <base>/.online/<key>,
32 * stat/access/open/unlink it locally, call the MSS only through `invoke`), so
33 * the whole vtable except destroy is implemented ONCE against this head
34 * (frm_mss_* below, defined in sd_frm_stub.c) and the adapters differ only in
35 * their invoker: posix_spawn of $BRIX_FRM_STAGECMD vs a dlsym'd call. */
