2 * client/lib/platform/tcp_rtt.c - the connection's smoothed RTT, host-free
3 *
4 * WHAT: brix_plat_tcp_rtt — one getsockopt into the host's TCP info struct,
5 * normalised to microseconds.
6 * WHY: Every host knows the same three facts (smoothed RTT, its variation,
7 * the retransmit count) and spells all of them differently. Keeping the
8 * body here and the spellings in <host>/host_net.h leaves one
9 * implementation to read and reason about, rather than a copy per host
10 * that has to be kept in step by eye.
11 * HOW: BRIX_PLAT_HOST_HEADER(host_net.h) resolves to this directory's
12 * <host>/host_net.h through the same computed include the rest of the
13 * PAL uses; that header names the struct, the socket option and the
14 * field-to-microseconds mapping.
15 */
