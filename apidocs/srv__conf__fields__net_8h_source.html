135 /* ---- durable async backend-op queue (brix_backend_async) ----
136 * When enabled, backend MUTATIONS (unlink/rmdir/rename/mkdir/write-commit) are
137 * routed through the per-worker durable coalescing queue instead of running
138 * inline: the op is journalled (fsync, survives reboot), the client is PARKED,
139 * and the batch is flushed in bulk to the backend when it reaches
140 * backend_async_batch ops OR backend_async_wait ms elapse — whichever first;
141 * the client is then answered with the real backend result (it may time out
