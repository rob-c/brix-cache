1/*
2 * brixcvmfs_rw_internal.h — private Phase-38 split contract for the writable
3 * CVMFS-brix overlay driver, shared ONLY between brixcvmfs_rw.c (core: state,
4 * path/layer helpers, open/read/write family) and brixcvmfs_rw_ext.c
5 * (directory + metadata ops, the ops table, mount lifecycle).
6 *
7 * WHAT: the process-global overlay state and the handful of core helpers/ops
8 * the ext TU calls after the union driver was split by concern.
9 * WHY: not a public API — the read-only driver still binds through
10 * brixcvmfs_internal.h's accessor seam. This header exists so the two rw
11 * TUs can reach one another's state/entry points without reintroducing a
12 * monolith. Behaviour is unchanged from the pre-split single file.
13 * HOW: each extern/prototype is DEFINED in brixcvmfs_rw.c. Single-threaded
14 * mount (-s), so the globals need no locking. See
15 * docs/refactor/phase-38-file-size-unix-modularity.md.
16 */
