4/*
5 * voms_io.h — the input and output bundles for VOMS attribute extraction.
6 *
7 * WHAT: brix_voms_in_t (the verified certificate chain to read) and
8 * brix_voms_out_t (the caller-owned buffers to fill).
9 * WHY: the stream (ngx_brix_module.h) and HTTP (voms_http.h) sides may not
10 * include each other's umbrella headers, yet both need these types; and
11 * bundling keeps brix_extract_voms_fqans() within the five-parameter
12 * limit as the output set grows.
13 * HOW: plain aggregates over <stddef.h> and OpenSSL's X509 types. Every
14 * output buffer is optional: a NULL pointer or a zero size means "the
15 * caller does not want this view", and the producers skip it.
16 *
17 * The three output views are NOT interchangeable:
18 * primary_vo the first VO name — log/metric safe (brix_vo_token_is_safe)
19 * vo_list every VO name, CSV — log/metric safe, the `g` selector's list
20 * fqan_list every raw FQAN, CSV — 2.0 F20; the ONLY source from which
21 * brix_identity_derive_attrs can recover a VOMS role, and NEVER a
22 * log field or a metric label (it carries '/' and '='; INVARIANT 8).
23 */
