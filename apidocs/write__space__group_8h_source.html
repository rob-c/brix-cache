1/*
2 * write_space_group.h — per-space-group accounting + write admission
3 * (phase-115 W3.3; the enforced half of `brix_oss_space ... quota=`).
4 *
5 * WHAT: resolves the group a handle's FINAL path belongs to, measures the
6 * bytes charged to a group, and refuses a data write that would push
7 * the group past its quota with the same kXR_overQuota the export-wide
8 * brix_oss_quota gate uses. kXR_Qspace reuses the measurement so the
9 * report and the gate never disagree.
10 *
11 * WHY: a per-VO cap is the whole point of space groups; an advertised quota
12 * nobody enforces is just a label.
13 *
14 * HOW: usage = a confined brix_vfs_walk of the prefix summing regular-file
15 * sizes (no allocation, RESOLVE_BENEATH), cached per worker for
16 * BRIX_SPACE_GROUP_TTL_MS and bumped by every admitted write so a burst
17 * inside the TTL cannot overrun the cap. A measurement failure admits
18 * the write (fail-open, like the export-wide gate: a quota is a policy
19 * cap, not an integrity gate) and is logged.
20 */
