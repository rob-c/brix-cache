1/* swarm_internal.h — cross-file seam for the split G12 swarm engine.
2 *
3 * WHAT: the membership types, per-process registration/context tables, and
4 * former-static entry points shared between swarm.c (registration,
5 * membership core, roster wire format, roster endpoint) and
6 * swarm_gossip.c (probe thread task, ring rebuild, gossip lifecycle,
7 * worker init).
8 * WHY: swarm.c grew past the file-size gate; the event-loop/thread-pool
9 * engine was lifted whole into swarm_gossip.c. Every symbol DEFINED in
10 * one file but CALLED from the other is declared here — no new state
11 * was introduced, the config-time statics merely became module-internal
12 * externs (same seam idiom as cvmfs_module_internal.h). Nothing here is
13 * part of the public cvmfs:// surface (that lives in cvmfs.h).
14 * HOW: include after cvmfs.h. Each declaration names its defining file.
