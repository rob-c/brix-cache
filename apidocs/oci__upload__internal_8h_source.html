1/*
2 * oci_upload_internal.h — the two halves of the blob upload machine.
3 *
4 * WHAT: the request-scoped session record and the small answer helpers shared
5 * between the routing half (oci_upload.c) and the body half
6 * (oci_upload_seal.c).
7 * WHY: nginx splits every body-carrying method in two — a handler that
8 * DECIDES and a callback that ACTS, with only the request between them.
9 * The record below is that "only the request": it is what the decision
10 * leaves behind for the callback to find. Keeping the two halves in
11 * separate translation units keeps each one readable; keeping their
12 * contract in one header keeps them honest about it.
13 */
