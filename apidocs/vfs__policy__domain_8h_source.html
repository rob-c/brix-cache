4/*
5 * vfs_policy_domain.h — the typed storage-domain assert (phase-107 C8/C9).
6 *
7 * WHAT: Declares the runtime half of the storage-domain claim: a caller states
8 * what it believes an instance's storage IS (brix_vfs_domain_t, defined
9 * next to the field it types in fs/backend/sd.h), and the assert checks
10 * the instance agrees before a service-storage mutation proceeds.
11 *
12 * WHY: Phase 105 drew the export/service line in prose and 108 free-text
13 * vfs-seam-allow comments; nothing enforced it at runtime. These two
14 * functions turn the paragraph into a refusal: service code pointed at
15 * export storage is a programming error and fails with EINVAL — loudly —
16 * where the export kernel's client-facing policy refusal is EROFS and
17 * discloses nothing. Conflating the two is how a service path acquires
18 * an export-shaped refusal nobody notices.
19 *
20 * HOW: brix_vfs_domain_mutation() checks the caller's claimed domain against
21 * inst->domain; EXPORT routes to the phase-105 kernel with the
22 * fail-closed policy. brix_vfs_service_mutation() is the narrow form —
23 * "this must be service storage at all" — for callers whose only claim
24 * is not-export (gcas dedup). A domain is a statement, never a grant.
25 *
26 * Requires nothing beyond its own includes; usable from fs/cache and every
27 * service-storage mutator.
28 */

36/* Assert `inst` belongs to `domain` before a mutation attributed to `op`.
37 * EXPORT delegates to the phase-105 kernel: an instance carries no request
38 * policy, so the only policy available is the fail-closed default and the
39 * result is EROFS — an export mutation must arrive through a policy-bearing
40 * form, never through a domain claim. Any other domain returns NGX_OK on a
41 * match, else NGX_ERROR with errno = EINVAL and one crit log line: a service
42 * path cannot launder an export mutation through a domain claim, and a
43 * zero-initialised instance is EXPORT, so the untaught site gets the strict
44 * domain. */
