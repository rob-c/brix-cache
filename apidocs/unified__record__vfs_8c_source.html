3/*
4 * unified_record_vfs.c — record-side mutators for the VFS mutation-surface
5 * metric families.
6 *
7 * WHAT: Implements the brix_metric_vfs_* record helpers the fs layer calls to
8 * bump the unified SHM counters for its mutation surface:
9 * _vfs_mutation_denied (phase-105 read-only refusals), the phase-107 C1
10 * writer-spill trio (_vfs_spill_bytes / _vfs_spill_refused /
11 * _vfs_spill_active), and the phase-107 C4 bulk-delete pair
12 * (_vfs_bulk_delete) — all declared in unified.h.
13 * WHY: The vfs family grew unified_record.c past the 600-line file budget
14 * (coding-standards §1); it is also the one recorder group keyed by the
15 * fs layer's own enums (mutate-op table, BRIX_FS_ID_COUNT driver ids)
16 * rather than by protocol-plane labels, so it forms its own unit the
17 * same way the exporter side split into unified_export/_io.
18 * HOW: Identical contract to the unified_record.c mutators: resolve the SHM
19 * block via brix_metrics_shared(), range-check every index against its
20 * compile-time-bounded enum, then bump with the lock-free BRIX_ATOMIC_*
21 * macros. No function here ever takes a path, subject, or key.
22 */

24/*
25 * brix_metric_vfs_mutation_denied — record one phase-105 read-only denial.
26 *
27 * WHAT: Bumps vfs_mutation_denied_total[proto][op]. No-op on an out-of-range
28 * protocol or operation, or on detached SHM.
29 *
30 * WHY: A read-only export that is silently refusing writes is indistinguishable

131/*
132 * brix_metric_vfs_spill_* — phase-107 C1 writer-spill telemetry.
133 *
134 * WHAT: spill_bytes adds `bytes` absorbed into the reorder scratch;
135 * spill_refused bumps one reordered upload the spill could not serve;
136 * spill_active moves the open-scratch gauge by +/-1 at create/discard.
137 *
138 * WHY: A site whose clients reorder (GridFTP mode E, multi-stream xrdcp) but
139 * whose spill root is missing or undersized fails uploads with ENOSPC;
