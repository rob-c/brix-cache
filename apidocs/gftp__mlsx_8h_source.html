4/*
5 * gftp_mlsx.h — client-role MLSD/MLST fact-line parser (RFC 3659 §7) for the
6 * outbound gsiftp:// storage driver.
7 *
8 * WHAT: invert one "facts SP pathname" line into the size/type/mtime/name the
9 * SD namespace layer maps onto brix_sd_stat_t / brix_sd_dirent_t.
10 *
11 * WHY: the phase-82 gateway only *emits* MLSx (ev_list_fill); a client must
12 * reverse the fact grammar (`type=;size=;modify=;perm=;unix.mode=;`). Directory
13 * listings from a remote origin are attacker-influenced, so the parser is
14 * defensive: unknown facts are ignored, a name carrying NUL/CR/LF or a path
15 * separator is rejected (the SD layer re-confines the survivor), and numeric
16 * facts reject overflow. Pure (no nginx types) so it is exhaustively testable.
17 *
18 * HOW: `gftp_mlsx_parse` splits the leading facts blob at the first space,
19 * walks the `;`-separated `key=value` facts case-insensitively, and returns the
20 * name as a pointer/length into the caller's line buffer (no copies).
21 */
