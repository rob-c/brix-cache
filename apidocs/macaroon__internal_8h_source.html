3/* Internal descriptors and cross-file entry points shared by the macaroon
4 * validation split (macaroon.c / macaroon_parse.c / macaroon_caveats.c /
5 * macaroon_crypto.c).
6 *
7 * WHAT: Declares the file-local structs threaded through one macaroon parse
8 * (the third-party-caveat capture record, the parse working state, and the
9 * invariant parse inputs) plus the five functions that cross a translation-unit
10 * boundary after the phase-79 file-size split: the packet-length helper, the
11 * HMAC chain parser, the path-caveat intersection, the first-party caveat
12 * classifier, and the AES-256-CBC vid decrypt.
13 *
14 * WHY: macaroon.c exceeded the ~500-line file-size guard, so it was carved into
15 * four cohesive units. The HMAC/packet machinery (macaroon_parse.c), the
16 * caveat/scope narrowing (macaroon_caveats.c), and the vid crypto
17 * (macaroon_crypto.c) each call — or are called by — the others. Grouping the
18 * shared structs and the crossing entry points here keeps every definition in
19 * exactly one place while preserving the identical bytes, HMAC ordering, and
20 * caveat semantics of the original single-file implementation.
21 *
22 * HOW: Requires token_internal.h (brix_token_claims_t / brix_token_scope_t,
23 * ngx types, u_char) and scopes.h (BRIX_SCOPE_PATH_MAX, brix_token_parse_scopes)
24 * before inclusion. brix_macaroon_tp_t records one third-party caveat captured
25 * during the root parse; brix_macaroon_parse_state_t is the per-parse working
26 * set; macaroon_parse_input_t bundles the invariant parse inputs (tp_arr != NULL
