4/*
5 * shutdown_hold.h — keep a retiring worker alive for parked XRootD handles.
6 *
7 * nginx's graceful worker loop exits once only cancelable timers remain. An
8 * authenticated XRootD keepalive deliberately has no idle read timer, but an
9 * open handle must survive a reload so a client can continue using that
10 * session on the retiring worker. The embedded timer is non-cancelable and
11 * is re-armed at a long cadence; the normal worker_shutdown_timeout remains
12 * the hard upper bound during shutdown.
13 */
