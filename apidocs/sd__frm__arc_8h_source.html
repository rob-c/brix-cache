1/*
2 * sd_frm_arc.h — dataset archiver MSS adapter (phase-115 W3.1, the OssArc analog)
3 *
4 * WHAT: a DECORATOR over any brix_mss_adapter_t (stub | exec | lib). Keys
5 * deeper than `depth` components belong to a DATASET (their first
6 * `depth` components); a dataset's files are written online one by one,
7 * and the moment its completion marker `.brix-dataset-complete` is
8 * committed the adapter seals every member into ONE store-only ZIP,
9 * migrates that single object, and writes an index sidecar. A later
10 * read of one member recalls the archive and extracts just that member.
11 *
12 * WHY: tape systems bill per object and HEP datasets are thousands of small
13 * files; stock XRootD's OssArc solves it the same way (zip per dataset +
14 * index). Doing it as a decorator keeps every real MSS dialect untouched.
15 *
16 * HOW: enabled by the `?arc=<depth>` query of the tape:// store URL
17 * (brix_sd_frm_parse_query). Archive key `<dataset>.brixarc.zip`
18 * (reserved: no client may create it); sidecar `<base>/.arcidx<dataset>.idx`
19 * (outside `.online`, so the purge engine never sees it). Sealed datasets
20 * are immutable: a new member is refused with EPERM, a second marker with
21 * EEXIST. Member names come from the online walk and are validated by
22 * brix_zip_name_ok on both seal and recall.
23 */
