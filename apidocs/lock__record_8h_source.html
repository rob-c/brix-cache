1/*
2 * lock_record.h — the persisted resource-lock record (phase-107 C7).
3 *
4 * WHAT: The on-disk format of a WebDAV lock — one pipe-delimited xattr value
5 * on the locked resource — as a protocol-neutral type: the xattr key,
6 * the record struct, encode/decode, and the ancestor-walk path helper.
7 *
8 * WHY: Phase-107 C7 makes lock coverage a VFS question
9 * (brix_vfs_require_unlocked): a lock taken over WebDAV must refuse an
10 * XRootD, GridFTP or S3 write. The VFS cannot include a protocol header,
11 * so the record format moved here from src/protocols/webdav (webdav.h /
12 * prop_xattr.c) byte-for-byte; WebDAV keeps its names as aliases and
13 * still owns the lock STATE MACHINE (LOCK/UNLOCK, refresh, discovery,
14 * reaping) — this unit owns only the format.
15 *
16 * HOW: Encoding is schema v2: `v=2|token=...|owner=...|expires=<wallclock
17 * seconds>|scope=exclusive|shared|depth=infinity|0|null=0|1`. The
18 * decoder forces a non-v2 record to expires=0 (already expired) so a
19 * legacy monotonic-msec deadline is released, never honoured.
20 */
