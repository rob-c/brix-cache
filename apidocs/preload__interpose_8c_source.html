1/*
2 * client/lib/platform/darwin/preload_interpose.c - the dyld interposing table
3 *
4 * WHAT: One __DATA,__interpose row per wrapped libc entry point, pairing the
5 * shim's brixposix_<name> with libSystem's <name>.
6 * WHY: dyld never lets an inserted library shadow libSystem by symbol name
7 * (two-level namespaces bind every call to the library it was linked
8 * against); this table is the supported way to redirect them, and it
9 * is applied to every image in the process except this one.
10 * HOW: BRIXPOSIX_WRAPPED (brixposix_internal.h) is the single list of what
11 * the shim interposes; a wrapper added there without a definition
12 * fails this object's link rather than silently doing nothing.
13 */
