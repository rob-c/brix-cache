1/*
2 * unified.h — cross-protocol metric vocabulary and record-side API.
3 *
4 * WHAT: Declares the enums that give EVERY protocol plane a single shared label
5 * vocabulary — brix_proto_t (generated from core/types/proto_list.h:
6 * stream/webdav/s3/cvmfs/gridftp), brix_metric_op_t (read/write/stat/
7 * delete/mkdir/rename/dirlist/tpc/xattr/copy), and brix_err_class_t
8 * (ok/not_found/forbidden/io_error/other) — plus the AUTH_*, TPC_*, and
9 * latency-bucket slot constants. It also declares the record-side helpers
10 * (brix_metric_op_done / _cache_result / _auth / _tpc) every protocol
11 * handler calls, and the name/classification helpers (proto/op/err/auth
12 * name lookups, errno→err and http-status→err mappers, auth-slot mapper).
13 * WHY: Per-protocol metric modules historically each defined their own labels,
14 * producing inconsistent dashboards. Funnelling every protocol through
15 * one enum set keeps Prometheus labels low-cardinality (INVARIANT #8) and
16 * lets a single exporter (brix_export_unified_metrics in unified.c) emit
17 * directly comparable brix_io_* / brix_auth_total / brix_tpc_* series.
18 * Adding a plane is one row in proto_list.h — never a new label surface.
19 * HOW: Enum members are dense 0-based so they index fixed-size SHM counter arrays
20 * directly; the trailing *_COUNT member sizes those arrays. The implementation
21 * lives in unified.c; consumers include this header to record an event with a
22 * proto/op/err triple rather than touching SHM fields by hand.
23 */

64/*
65 * Cache-disposition vocabulary (phase-106 W1, made the shared vocabulary in

68 * unchanged. NEGHIT is the one brix extension (negative-cache hit) and must not

70 * deliberately not consulted for this request". NONE renders "-": no cache
