4 * A wrapper is named brixposix_<name>; darwin/preload_interpose.c pairs it
5 * with libSystem's symbol in a __DATA,__interpose table, which dyld applies
6 * to every image except the one carrying the table. A plain reference to
7 * `name` from inside the shim therefore IS the real function (and, unlike a
