4/*
5 * n2n_stage.h — the ctx-carried logical↔physical name-translation stage
6 * (phase-108 A.4).
7 *
8 * WHAT: Two thin wrappers that bind an export's resolved brix_n2n_cfg_t (carried
9 * on the VFS ctx as ctx->n2n) to the pure translators in site_n2n.c —
10 * brix_n2n_lfn2pfn() / brix_n2n_pfn2lfn(). LFN→PFN is what a backend keys
11 * storage by; PFN→LFN renders a physical listing back in logical terms.
12 * WHY: The pure translators are cfg-in / string-out and know nothing of a
13 * request. This is the single seam that reads the per-export cfg off the
14 * ctx, so every plane composes one name the same way and a NULL cfg means
15 * IDENTITY by construction rather than by each caller remembering to.
16 * HOW: ctx->n2n (NULL ⇒ a static IDENTITY cfg) is handed straight to the pure
17 * function. The wrappers add NO translation logic — they are the ctx→cfg
18 * adapter and nothing more; canonicalization and ".." rejection remain the
19 * pure canonicalizer's job. SECURITY: an LFN reaches here only AFTER
20 * resolve_path() has confined it (INVARIANT #4); the wrappers do not
21 * confine, they translate a name already proven in-export.
22 */
